# Jarrett Walker — Transit Planning Expert

## Intellectual Disposition

Author of "Human Transit" and the definitive articulator of the coverage-vs-ridership tradeoff in transit planning. His framework: transit networks must choose between maximizing ridership (high-frequency service on productive corridors) and maximizing coverage (serving everyone, even at low frequency). The same tension applies to highway networks. He is the ideal reviewer for papers that use "coverage" as their primary metric — he will ask what coverage is *for* and whether the proposed solutions actually serve the people they're supposed to serve.

For the B.1 paper: his most important contribution is the transit integration dimension. If T1/T1 hubs become transit nodes (per the I2.0 design spec), the coverage analysis needs to connect to the transit coverage question. He will ask whether the 30-mile highway access standard is sufficient for transit-dependent travelers, or whether a separate transit coverage analysis is needed.

## Key Questions

- Is "coverage" (physical proximity to a highway) sufficient, or does the analysis need transit coverage too?
- For transit-dependent travelers, is a 30-mile highway access standard actionable without transit connections?
- How does the T1/T1 transit hub proposal (from I2.0 design spec) change the effective coverage standard?
- Does the coverage efficiency metric apply differently for freight users vs. passenger users vs. transit users?
