### PP2.1 — EIT designation authority (§5311(f) connection)

The original concern: the Essential Intercity Transportation designation was presented as a new authorization requirement without grounding in existing statutory authority, making the policy recommendation weaker than it needed to be.

The revision reframes the EIT designation as EAS-analogous (49 U.S.C. § 41731) and explicitly notes that 49 U.S.C. § 5311(f) provides existing authority for rural intercity bus funding without requiring new legislation. This is a meaningful improvement. The §5311(f) connection is more actionable than an EAS analogy: Section 5311(f) funding already exists, is administered through state DOTs, and targets exactly the rural communities identified in this paper's equity analysis — communities with no viable intercity transit option. A new EIT designation would require Surface Transportation Reauthorization; §5311(f) expansion could be accomplished through appropriations.

The near-term policy vehicle for what this paper recommends is §5311(f) expansion, not a new designation. The revision correctly identifies this connection. A recommendation to highlight §5311(f) expansion as the actionable near-term mechanism — rather than framing the equity section primarily around a hypothetical new designation — would strengthen the policy chapter.
