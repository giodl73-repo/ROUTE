# Current Goal: I-80 Flagship Stabilization

## Mission

Complete one defensible ROUTE result from source-backed corridor measurement
through gap diagnosis, treatment selection, adversarial review, and a concise
decision packet.

The anchor is I-80. ROUTE will not expand to new countries, new doctrine
families, or placeholder-only evidence ledgers until the anchor exit gates are
closed or explicitly held.

## Why This Matters

ROUTE has broad implementation, maps, research, review machinery, and evidence
controls. Its highest-value next step is depth: prove that another person can
follow one corridor from raw evidence to a bounded decision without navigating
the entire repository.

## Active Wave

`waves/2026-07-11-i80-flagship-stabilization/WAVE.md`

## Success Criteria

- `corpus/existing/i80.md` has no human-annotation placeholders and names every
  material source, estimate, limitation, and rubric version.
- The I-80 anchor has a corridor-specific gap record that distinguishes measured
  failures, source gaps, model limitations, and geometry artifacts.
- One bounded Interstate 2.0 treatment is selected from evidence rather than
  assumed in advance.
- The treatment receives all seven Parliament voices plus citation, numeracy,
  and scope review.
- A compact decision packet can be regenerated and presented in ten minutes.
- At least three external reviewers are identified for the next validation
  round: a DOT/MPO practitioner, a freight operator or economist, and a
  transportation researcher.
- A clean clone has a reproducible dependency and CI path for the flagship
  command bundle.

## Opening Rule

No new geographic expansion, international adapter, doctrine family, or
placeholder-only ledger is in scope. Work that does not strengthen the I-80
flagship, its reproducibility, or its external review path is deferred.

## Immediate Work

1. Establish the flagship baseline and reconcile the live planning documents.
2. Complete the I-80 existing-corridor record.
3. Produce an I-80-specific gap and treatment decision.
4. Run Parliament and editorial review on the selected treatment.
5. Build the compact flagship packet and external-review docket.
6. Harden dependency pinning, CI, and the command path used by the packet.
