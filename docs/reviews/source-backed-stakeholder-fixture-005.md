# Source-Backed Stakeholder Fixture 005

## Purpose

This fixture closes one bounded community, environmental, and local-impact
source-backed example for the industry/stakeholder fixture campaign.

It uses FHWA social-environment project-delivery material and EPA transportation
air-pollution material to show that community impacts, social environment,
vehicle/engine pollutants, health effects, and environmental concerns must
remain visible in ROUTE communications and review artifacts.

This fixture does not create community, local-government, FHWA, USDOT, EPA,
environmental organization, public-health, or agency endorsement. It does not
prove environmental clearance, mitigation adequacy, community support,
public-involvement sufficiency, air-quality compliance, health outcome,
construction readiness, guaranteed service, numeric ROI, approval, public
readiness, or external readiness.
