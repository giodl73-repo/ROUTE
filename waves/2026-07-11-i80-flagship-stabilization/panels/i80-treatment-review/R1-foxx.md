# R1 - Foxx

Verdict: **hold and narrow**

## Findings

- **BLOCK** - Independent connector concepts lacked displacement and
  environmental-justice constraints.
- **WARN** - Unrestricted I-235 or local-road diversion could transfer diesel,
  noise, safety, and access burdens to urban communities.
- **WARN** - The road-only package omitted non-driving access and intermodal
  alternatives.
- **NOTE** - Zero loaded demand concealed who benefits and who carries the
  burden.

## Disposition

The validation plan now requires displacement, community-health, non-driving,
upstream-diversion, and intermodal gates before any conceptual path advances.
