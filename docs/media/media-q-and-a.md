# ROUTE Media Q&A

## Purpose

This Q&A gives reporters and editors short answers they can use when describing
ROUTE under deadline.

It is not a press release, public launch, official statement, external
rehearsal, agency review, construction proposal, funding request, ROI finding,
eligibility finding, compliance finding, endorsement record, or approval record.

## Short Answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is ROUTE? | ROUTE is an evidence-bounded research and tooling project for exploring an Interstate 2.0 service hierarchy for roads. |
| What is Interstate 2.0? | In this repo, it is a service-network vision: roads grouped by role, such as national spine, regional connector, feeder/access, and terminal/local access. |
| Is this an official plan? | No. The materials do not claim official-plan status or agency adoption. |
| Is ROUTE asking government to build specific projects? | No. Current materials do not make construction recommendations. |
| Are the service windows guarantees? | No. They are planning targets used to compare service roles until reliability evidence closes. |
| What are the maps for? | Maps make the service structure visible. They do not prove construction, SLA, terminal, asset, environmental, or ROI readiness. |
| Does ROUTE prove ROI? | No. ROI and cost are treated as evidence contracts; numeric ROI claims remain gated. |
| Are agencies or stakeholders endorsing this? | No endorsement or approval claim is made. |
| Why mention Port NOLA? | ROUTE uses a public Port NOLA road-access source as one bounded example of source custody and claim holds, not as endorsement or proof of terminal performance. |
| What does the Canada pilot prove? | It proves internally that ROUTE can move a non-U.S. country pilot through a source-backed evidence-gated adapter workflow. It does not prove an official Canadian network, approval, SLA, ROI, public readiness, or external validation. |
| What is the strongest current claim? | ROUTE has a coherent evidence-bounded communications package and nine bounded source-backed fixtures for internal rehearsal. External/public readiness remains held. |

## If You Quote One Sentence

Use:

> ROUTE is an evidence-bounded research and tooling project exploring how a
> future Interstate 2.0 could organize roads by service role.

Do not use:

> ROUTE is a plan to build the next interstate system.

## Verification Checklist For Reporters

Before relying on a stronger claim, check:

| Check | Pass Condition |
|---|---|
| Is the claim about an official plan, agency approval, funding, construction, or compliance? | Verify outside this repo; do not infer it from ROUTE docs. |
| Is the claim numeric? | Require source path, units, date/year, method, uncertainty, and numeracy review. |
| Is the claim based on a map? | Treat the map as structural unless a source-backed evidence row says otherwise. |
| Is the claim about ROI or cost? | Look for a source pack and numeracy review; otherwise describe ROI as gated. |
| Is the claim about stakeholder validation? | Look for a source-backed fixture and affected role review; otherwise say stakeholder validation is not claimed. |
| Is the claim about a service window? | Describe it as a planning target, not a guarantee. |
| Is the claim about Port NOLA? | Say the public source supports one bounded terminal-access fixture only; do not imply endorsement or terminal performance. |

## Media Response Patterns

| If Asked | Say |
|---|---|
| "What is new here?" | The repo now has media resources that separate the Interstate 2.0 concept from evidence-gated claims. |
| "Is this ready for public rollout?" | The materials are readable, but public-readiness and external-readiness claims remain held. |
| "Can I use the maps?" | Yes as structural visuals with captions and holds; no as proof of readiness. |
| "Can I say this solves freight reliability?" | No. You can say it frames freight reliability as a planning and evidence problem. |
| "Can I say it helps rural access?" | You can say T3/T4 keep rural and production access visible; do not say access problems are solved. |
| "Can I say it has real source-backed examples?" | Yes, nine bounded fixtures exist for internal rehearsal and evidence discipline. Do not describe them as external validation. |
| "Can I say Canada is proven?" | Say Canada is internally proven as a source-backed adapter workflow; do not say it is externally validated or officially adopted. |

## Corrections Policy For This Package

If a media statement conflicts with `docs/reports/route-evidence-posture.md`,
the evidence posture controls.

If a source, number, map, agency position, stakeholder statement, or readiness
claim is not traceable in the repo, treat it as unverified.
