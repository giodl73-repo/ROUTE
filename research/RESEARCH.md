# ROUTE — Research

Interstate 2.0: data-driven corridor analysis, gap detection, freight reliability, and national highway network design — including a transit passenger layer enabled by the highway investment.

See [MODULE.md](MODULE.md) for full track design, causal chains, and quantification contracts.

## Research Tracks

| Track | Theme | Papers |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Corpus & Scoring** | Calibrate the measurement instrument from 227 existing corridors | A.1, A.2 |
| **B — Gap Analysis** | Missing links, capacity bottlenecks, resilience holes, T1/T1 intersection vulnerabilities | B.1, B.2, B.3, B.4 |
| **C — Freight & Throughput** | O-D reliability, transit time, national max-flow | C.1, C.2 |
| **D — Resilience** | Climate exposure, incident economics, redundancy value | D.1, D.2 |
| **E — Interstate 2.0 Design** | Managed lanes, intermodal, EV, full I2.0 framework | E.1, E.2 |
| **F — Transit Integration** | T1/T1 hubs as transit nodes; intercity bus corridor coverage and equity | F.1, F.2 |
