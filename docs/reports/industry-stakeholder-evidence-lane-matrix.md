# ROUTE Industry And Stakeholder Evidence-Lane Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix answers a narrow question: which industry and stakeholder lanes are
represented in the current ROUTE communications package, and what evidence
supports that representation?

It is not an industry validation record. It does not show endorsement, agency
approval, public readiness, external rehearsal readiness, official-plan status,
construction readiness, guaranteed service, numeric ROI, eligibility, or
compliance.

## Current Verdict

Decision: **representation_shown_validation_held**

The package represents the major review lanes needed for an Interstate 2.0
communications rehearsal. The strongest support is internal: `.roles` coverage,
role-review findings, source-pack discipline, media claim controls, and one
bounded Port NOLA terminal-access fixture.

That is enough for media, sponsor, and internal dry-run language that says
ROUTE has represented lanes and a source-gated workflow. It is not enough to say
that industries, agencies, communities, or affected users have validated the
plan.

| Lane | Safe Claim | Current Evidence | Current Boundary | Next Source-Backed Fixture |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Validation Language

Use these phrases:

- "represented in the review lanes"
- "covered by `.roles` and role review"
- "source-gated"
- "one bounded terminal-access fixture"
- "internal dry-run pass with external rehearsal held"
- "requires source custody before promotion"

Avoid these phrases:

- "validated by industry"
- "endorsed by stakeholders"
- "agency-approved"
- "ready to build"
- "guaranteed service"
- "positive ROI"
- "public-ready"
- "external-ready"

## Promotion Ladder

| Step | Meaning | What It Allows |
|---|---|---|
| Lane represented | A role, report, template, or review surface names the stakeholder concern. | Internal and media-safe language that the concern is represented. |
| Internal role review | `.roles` or a review record applies the relevant lens and records pass, risk, hold, or fail. | Internal rehearsal use with stated boundaries. |
| Source-needed item | A specific source gap, intake question, or fixture candidate is named. | Work planning and reviewer challenge, not claim promotion. |
| Source-backed fixture | A named source, requirement row, artifact effect, and review result are recorded. | Bounded example language limited to that fixture. |
| Affected-lane re-review | The stakeholder and editorial lanes re-check the changed artifact and claims. | Stronger internal use if the review passes without new holds. |
| External validation | A named outside participant, venue, source permission, role review, and closeout record exist. | Only the specific validated claim, audience, and venue may be described externally. |

## Current Gaps

| Gap | Why It Matters | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Five populated source-backed fixtures exist. | They demonstrate process for terminal access, freight parking/rest constraints, rural/agricultural access lanes, state delivery controls, and community/environmental impact lanes, not cross-industry agreement. | Use `docs/reports/industry-stakeholder-source-fixture-campaign.md` to add more fixture packs by lane before stronger claims. |
| Most lanes are represented by roles and reports, not outside participants. | Role simulation is useful for pressure testing but cannot stand in for affected-party review. | Keep external validation language held. |
| ROI, cost, reliability, asset, hazard, and local-impact claims remain source-specific. | These are the claims most likely to drift into unsupported proof language. | Require source packs and numeracy/domain review before promotion. |
| Media materials exist before public readiness is closed. | Reporters may find the repo and overstate it. | Point media users to this matrix, the source index, and evidence posture. |

## Gate

Decision: **pass_with_risk for representation; hold validation**

Rationale: ROUTE can safely say that its communications package represents
freight, terminal, rural, state DOT, community/environmental, transit,
resilience, funder, technical, and media lanes. ROUTE cannot yet say those lanes
have validated the plan. Stronger statements require lane-specific source
fixtures, affected-role review, and external closeout.
