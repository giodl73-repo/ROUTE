# ROUTE Media Claim Guide

## Purpose

This guide helps media readers use ROUTE materials without turning a research
and tooling project into a stronger public claim than the evidence supports.

Use this when writing captions, summaries, sidebars, Q&A, explainers, or
background sections.

## Claim Levels

| Level | Meaning | Media Use |
|---|---|---|
| Story-ready | Safe as a concept or framing idea. | May be described as a vision, proposal, framework, or research framing. |
| Implemented | Supported by current code, command output, or repo artifact. | May be described with artifact context and limitations. |
| Heuristic | Useful but based on proxy data, simplified assumptions, or incomplete sources. | Must be labeled as heuristic, illustrative, or planning-stage. |
| Gated | Needs a source, command, review, validation, or role closeout. | Describe as required work, not achieved result. |
| Held | Must not be promoted. | Mention only as a boundary or blocker. |

## Safe Substitutions

| Instead Of | Use |
|---|---|
| "ROUTE's plan" | "ROUTE's research framework" |
| "will build" | "would examine" or "would test" |
| "guarantees 48-hour freight" | "uses 48-hour freight as a planning target" |
| "proves the map is ready" | "shows a structural service map with claim holds" |
| "validated by stakeholders" | "designed to accept stakeholder requirements through source custody and role review" |
| "positive ROI" | "ROI remains an evidence contract until source packs and numeracy review close" |
| "approved by DOT" | "not approved or endorsed by any DOT unless separately verified outside this repo" |

## Common Media Questions

| Question | Reliable Answer |
|---|---|
| Is Interstate 2.0 a government plan? | No. In this repo it is a service-network vision and research frame. |
| Is ROUTE asking to build new highways? | The current materials do not make construction recommendations. |
| Are the time promises guaranteed? | No. They are planning targets used to compare service roles. |
| Are the maps publishable proof? | No. Maps can explain structure, but claim-specific evidence remains separate. |
| Is there ROI? | No numeric ROI claim is promoted. ROI/cost work is framed as an evidence contract. |
| Has a stakeholder validated the plan? | No endorsement or validation claim is made. One bounded source-backed fixture demonstrates evidence discipline for internal rehearsal. |
| Can I cite Port NOLA as a supporter? | No. The Port NOLA source is used only as public road-access information for a bounded terminal-access fixture. |

## Caption Pattern

Use a caption like:

> ROUTE schematic map, used as a structural service visualization. The map does
> not prove construction readiness, operating SLA, terminal access, asset
> condition, ROI, or agency approval.

Do not use a caption like:

> ROUTE map of the future interstate buildout.

## Before Publishing A Stronger Claim

Do not publish stronger claims from ROUTE materials unless the claim has:

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| Source path, title, owner, date/year, and access note | Prevents vague data attribution. |
| Evidence label | Shows whether the claim is story-ready, implemented, heuristic, gated, or held. |
| Role review | Preserves scope, citation, numeracy, stakeholder, map, and delivery concerns. |
| Validation status | Separates docs/internal rehearsal from release or public-readiness claims. |
| Non-claim boundary | States what the source or artifact does not prove. |

## Red Lines

Do not infer:

- official adoption;
- construction readiness;
- guaranteed service;
- numeric ROI;
- statutory eligibility;
- regulatory compliance;
- agency endorsement;
- stakeholder validation;
- public-readiness;
- map proof;
- terminal-access proof beyond the bounded source row.

## Gate

Decision: **media_claims_guardrail**

Rationale: Media materials are useful only if they reduce claim drift. This
guide makes the safe language and red lines explicit without promoting external,
public, construction, SLA, ROI, eligibility, compliance, endorsement, approval,
or publication-readiness claims.
