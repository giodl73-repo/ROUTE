# I-11 Extended North — Pacific Coast Inland

## Overview

The gap between I-5 (Pacific Coast) and I-15 (Interior West) is the longest uninterrupted N-S gap in the western US — approximately 300 miles wide in Oregon and Washington. US-97 runs the full length from Weed CA north through Bend OR, the Columbia River Gorge area, Yakima WA, and to the Canadian border at Oroville WA.

The existing I-11 designation (Henderson NV to Boulder City NV, about 15 miles, plus US-93/US-95 corridor studies for Las Vegas-Phoenix) provides a natural southern anchor. Extending I-11 northward along the US-97 alignment would create a continuous Pacific inland corridor from the Arizona/Mexico border to Canada.

**Why I-11**: The number already exists in the SW corner of this corridor (Las Vegas area). I-11 was designated specifically for the Las Vegas-Phoenix-Tucson corridor. Extending it north through Oregon/Washington along US-97 completes the western inland corridor from Mexico to Canada — a natural extension of the existing concept.

## Strategic Case

1. **Cascade Mountains bypass**: I-5 through Seattle and Portland is frequently congested and subject to weather closures in winter. US-97/I-11 provides an inland bypass that avoids the Cascades west side and the urban congestion. This is already a heavily-used truck alternative.

2. **Agricultural irrigation corridor**: The Columbia River Basin (WA and OR east of Cascades) is one of the most productive agricultural zones in the US — apples, hops, wine grapes, potatoes, wheat. Current export routing uses I-82/I-84 east to I-84 to Portland. A direct I-11 northward would provide more direct access to Pacific NW ports.

3. **Yakima agricultural hub**: Yakima WA (on US-97) is the center of the Pacific Northwest fruit industry (apples, cherries, hops). An I-11 upgrade through Yakima would be the most impactful single freight infrastructure improvement for Pacific NW agriculture.

4. **Nevada-Oregon energy corridor**: The Great Basin has significant geothermal and solar potential being developed. Grid-scale energy infrastructure development in Nevada and Oregon will require construction material movement. I-11 northward would support this energy corridor.

5. **Military installations**: Fairchild AFB (Spokane WA), Whidbey Island NAS (WA), and the former Klamath Falls ANGB (OR) are in or near this corridor.
