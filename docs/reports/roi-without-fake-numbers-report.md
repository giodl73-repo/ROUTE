# ROI Without Fake Numbers

## Research Question

How should ROUTE explain investment value before source-backed benefits, costs,
price year, uncertainty, exclusions, and role review exist?

## Decision This Supports

This report supports the communications decision to make ROI discipline a
selling point. ROUTE should not hide the absence of numeric ROI. It should show
why premature ROI would be irresponsible and what evidence would make a future
benefit-cost claim reviewable.

## Executive Thesis

The honest ROI story is stronger than a fake number.

Interstate 2.0 is an investable idea because it can be broken into service
promises, candidate packages, benefit classes, cost classes, source labels,
uncertainty, and stakeholder review. It is not investable because a deck invents
a benefit-cost ratio before the model exists.

```text
value hypothesis
  -> benefit and cost classes
  -> source and price-year rules
  -> uncertainty and exclusions
  -> role review
  -> fundable next evidence step
```

## Findings

### ROUTE-ROI-01 - ROI is a gate, not a slogan.

**Observed constraint:** Current materials define benefit and cost classes, but
no source-backed summary values.

**Implication:** Funder materials should say ROUTE can build the evidence
contract for ROI, not that any corridor or hub has positive ROI.

**Confidence:** High.

### ROUTE-ROI-02 - Price year and scope are mandatory before money appears.

**Observed constraint:** The ROI/cost framework requires price year, time
horizon, geographic scope, included/excluded benefits, and cost basis.

**Implication:** Any future dollar figure without those fields should stay out
of decks and reports.

**Confidence:** High.

### ROUTE-ROI-03 - Negative or marginal results must be allowed.

**Observed constraint:** A credible ROI method must be able to hold, downgrade,
or reject an option.

**Implication:** ROUTE should sell comparability and decision quality, not
guaranteed positive outcomes.

**Confidence:** High.

### ROUTE-ROI-04 - Non-monetized concerns still need review.

**Observed constraint:** Rural access, community health, environmental impact,
delivery feasibility, and non-driving access cannot be collapsed into a single
net benefit without review.

**Implication:** ROI packages must preserve dissent rows and excluded-benefit
notes instead of hiding tradeoffs.

**Confidence:** Medium-high.

## What Counts Before Numbers

| Layer | Required Before Promotion |
|---|---|
| Benefit class | Mechanism, source family, confidence label, included/excluded status. |
| Cost class | Capital/lifecycle/operating boundary, price year, owner, exclusions. |
| Scenario | Baseline, adverse case, sensitivity rule, and failure handling. |
| Geography | State, corridor, hub, zone, or national scope. |
| Identity | Stable bundle, segment, hub, package, or explicitly held transitional id. |
| Review | Freight, numeracy, citation, state delivery, stakeholder, and scope lanes. |

## Safe Language

| Use This | Avoid This |
|---|---|
| "ROI starts as an evidence contract." | "This corridor has positive ROI." |
| "The next fundable step is a source pack and scenario package." | "The business case is already proven." |
| "Benefits and costs need price-year and uncertainty rules." | "The cost is known." |
| "Weak or negative results are valid outcomes." | "Every package will pay for itself." |
| "Excluded benefits and dissent stay visible." | "One number resolves all tradeoffs." |

## Evidence Needed To Promote A Future ROI Claim

| Evidence Need | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Price-year rule | Prevents incompatible cost and benefit values from being combined. |
| Benefit source pack | Separates narrative value from measurable value. |
| Cost source pack | Names capital, delivery, operating, maintenance, mitigation, and risk scope. |
| Uncertainty treatment | Shows ranges, sensitivity, and weak cases instead of false precision. |
| Scenario evidence | Tests whether the option survives disruption, demand, and operating assumptions. |
| Role review | Keeps freight, rural, state, community, environmental, and numeracy concerns visible. |

## Recommendations

### Adopt Now

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| Present ROI discipline as part of ROUTE's value. | communications owner / Numeracy Checker | Decks and briefs contain no ROI numbers. |
| Use the ROI gate before any future cost or benefit claim. | Freight Economist / Citation Auditor | Source, price-year, uncertainty, and exclusions are visible. |
| Fund the source pack before asking for construction money. | funder brief owner | Funder ask remains planning, evidence, demo, and pilot oriented. |

### Reject Or Defer

| Claim / Action | Reason |
|---|---|
| Publishing numeric ROI now. | Sources, price year, uncertainty, exclusions, and review are not closed. |
| Ranking corridors by invented benefit-cost ratio. | Comparable option model does not exist yet. |
| Treating community or rural impacts as resolved by net benefits. | Stakeholder review and dissent are required before promotion. |

## Non-Goals

- This report does not estimate costs, benefits, ROI, benefit-cost ratio, or
  funding need.
- This report does not select corridors, hubs, packages, or construction scope.
- This report does not claim official endorsement or agency readiness.

## Gate

Decision: pass_with_risk

Rationale: ROUTE can safely explain ROI as an evidence contract and funding
sequence. Any numeric ROI, cost, benefit, positive business-case, or construction
claim remains blocked until the named evidence gates close.
