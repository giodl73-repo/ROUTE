# Proposed I-31 — Central Plains Mexico-Canada Corridor

## Overview

The central plains corridor fills the 500-mile geographic gap between I-25 (Rocky Mountain front) and I-35 (Mississippi Plains) with a direct Mexico-to-Canada north-south arterial. Currently no interstate-standard road connects Eagle Pass TX (the 4th-largest US-Mexico land crossing by trade value, fastest growing at +20%/yr) directly to the Canadian border through the central Great Plains.

The proposed alignment follows US-287 from the Fort Worth TX area northward through Amarillo TX, across the Colorado eastern plains, through Cheyenne/Laramie WY, and into Montana. The southern portion would connect Eagle Pass TX through the Texas Hill Country and Panhandle.

**Why I-31**: The number sits cleanly between I-29 (eastern Dakotas, 1,499 miles east) and I-25 (Rocky Mountain front, 2,126 miles west). The existing I-27 stub (Lubbock-Amarillo, 121 miles) could be absorbed as the southern Texas extension.

## Strategic Case

1. **Mexico-Canada trade corridor**: Eagle Pass to Havre MT is ~2,200 miles. Currently all central plains Mexico-Canada freight routes I-35 (east) or I-25 (west), adding 400-600 miles.

2. **FE Warren AFB/USSTRATCOM**: The Air Force's nuclear missile command (USSTRATCOM at Offutt AFB NE, FE Warren AFB WY) relies on roads along this corridor. The Wyoming segment is the primary approach to the nation's ICBM fields (Minuteman III silos across Wyoming, Nebraska, Montana).

3. **Agricultural export dual-port access**: A truck leaving Amarillo TX can reach either Gulf Coast ports (Houston 500mi, Corpus Christi 600mi) OR Pacific Northwest ports (Tacoma/Portland via I-90, 1,400mi). No other corridor provides this bidirectional export optionality for the central plains.

4. **Northern Tier connector**: The I-31 terminus near Havre MT connects directly to the proposed I-92 (Northern Tier) — creating a true T1/T1 junction where none currently exists in the northern plains.

## Interstate 2.0 Features Required

- Managed freight lanes (2 per direction) on the Amarillo-Denver segment (highest volume)
- Enhanced rest areas every 100 miles (extremely sparse current coverage)
- EV charging corridor the full length (currently nearly zero in Wyoming/Montana segments)
- Resilience spurs every 50 miles (no US highway alternatives for 100+ mile stretches in WY/MT)
- Diamond intersection at I-40 (Amarillo TX) — T1/T1 node that doesn't currently exist
