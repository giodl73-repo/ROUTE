## P2 — Important (address in same revision cycle)

- [ ] **P2.1** Show explicit arithmetic decomposition of the $8.2B annual reliability cost: (a) annual freight VMT by segment, (b) loaded truck-hours experiencing PTI > 1.0, (c) ATRI cost rate ($/hour), (d) reliability premium multiplier. - [ ] **P2.2** Add commodity-weighted reliability cost discussion: acknowledge that uniform value-of-reliability treatment overstates benefit for bulk freight and understates it for high-value JIT freight. Either produce a commodity-weighted estimate or provide an explicit caveat bounding the error. *(McKinnon)*

- [ ] **P2.3** Add a carrier-level worked example: annual dollar benefit of PTI 1.86→1.15 for a carrier operating NY-LA 5x/week, net of managed lane toll costs. Demonstrate that the I2.0 investment produces a net shipper-level benefit, not just a corridor aggregate statistic. *(Neumark)*

- [ ] **P2.4** State managed lane toll assumption ($/mile or $/trip) and estimate freight carrier take-up rate. If toll costs offset reliability gains for some freight segments, bound the effective coverage of the reliability benefit. *(Neumark)*

- [ ] **P2.5** Add O-D pair selection rationale: either (a) compare candidate O-D pairs (minimum 4–6) ranked by annual reliability cost or PTI, demonstrating NY-LA and HOU-CHI are the highest-priority; or (b) explicitly reframe study as "two illustrative cases" rather than a claim of highest-priority status. *(Hanson)*

- [ ] **P2.6** Address directional flow imbalance on I-80: state whether PTI calculation and reliability cost reflect directional or bidirectional flows; quantify eastbound vs. westbound truck volume asymmetry and its effect on the reliability cost estimate. *(Hanson)*

- [ ] **P2.7** Add I-69 implementation status section: (a) miles built to freeway standard by state, (b) miles requiring new construction by state, (c) current EIS status for unprogrammed segments, (d) earliest realistic full-corridor completion under current authorization. Condition the +18% Gulf-Chicago max-flow result on full corridor completion. *(Puentes)*

- [ ] **P2.8** Acknowledge air freight competition for NY-LA high-value freight. Characterize what fraction of $5.7B reliability cost is attributable to freight segments competing with air, and whether those segments would realistically use I2.0 managed lanes. *(Hanson, Puentes)*
