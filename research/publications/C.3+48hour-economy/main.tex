\begin{abstract}
The difference between 3.6 days and 1.8 days on the New York--Los Angeles corridor is not
a matter of degree. It is a change of category. A product with a 72-hour shelf life has no
choice but to fly coast-to-coast today; four-day truck is simply not an option. Air freight
costs \$3--5 per pound. Truck costs \$0.15--0.25 per pound. The 15--20$\times$ cost premium
is not a shipper preference --- it is a structural constraint imposed by transit time.

A Monte Carlo simulation of 10,000 coast-to-coast trips under Interstate 2.0 managed lanes
with a driver relay network shows that 99.5\% of shipments complete the 2,790-mile
New York--Los Angeles journey in under 48 hours (p95 = 45.4 hours). The relay network
requires \$40 million in station infrastructure --- 0.02\% of the \$253 billion I2.0
portfolio --- and eliminates 30 hours of mandatory rest stops by cycling fresh regional
drivers at eight intermediate hubs rather than resting a single driver.

Five economic transformations follow from the 48-hour threshold. Air freight substitution
along coast-to-coast corridors yields an estimated \$8 billion per year in logistics cost
reduction. California's premium perishable agriculture --- strawberries, avocados, Pacific
seafood --- can reach New York fresh by refrigerated truck rather than by cargo hold,
worth \$2--4 billion annually. E-commerce retailers can serve the nation from two coastal
fulfillment hubs, avoiding \$4--20 billion in redundant warehouse capital. Pharmaceutical
cold-chain drugs stable at 2--8\,\textdegree C for 72 hours can truck instead of fly.
And the relay driver model creates 20,000 regional driving jobs --- positions held by
workers who sleep at home every night --- directly addressing the 80,000-driver shortage
that is the trucking industry's most binding constraint.

The 48-hour corridor is not a transportation story. It is an economic geography story.
It is about which supply chains become possible, which businesses become viable, and which
workers can hold careers in freight without sacrificing their families.
\end{abstract}
