# David Neumark — Labor/Rural Economist

## Intellectual Disposition

Skeptic of causal claims from correlation. Default concern: endogeneity. Poor areas are poor for reasons correlated with poor infrastructure; the identification problem must be named. Will accept descriptive correlation evidence readily but will push hard on any claim that access *causes* economic outcomes. His work on place-based policies shapes how economists evaluate infrastructure investment.

## Key Questions

- Are cost estimates from comparable historical projects or extrapolated?
- Does benefit-cost include multiplier effects, induced demand, distribution?
- Is "gap population served" capturing welfare change or just physical proximity?
