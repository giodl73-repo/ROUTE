# ROUTE DCR Express Payment Policy 001

## Purpose

The DCR can simulate operator payments for faster, verified, or priority
service. This is useful because some operators, carriers, ports, managed-lane
owners, or sponsors may pay for earlier review, verified windows, or more
reliable service posture.

The policy boundary is strict: ROUTE does not set official prices, collect
payments, define refund terms, guarantee revenue, guarantee SLA performance, or
prove public readiness.

## Simulated Tiers

| Tier | Simulated Price | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard | $0 | Baseline advisory route and status readout. |
| Priority advisory | $45 | Simulate willingness to pay for faster operator-reviewed guidance. |
| Verified window | $120 | Simulate premium demand for a verified service-window advisory. |

The cockpit may show paid-request counts and a revenue proxy. Those values are
simulation outputs, not a forecast, budget, invoice, toll, tariff, guarantee,
or ROI proof. The express-payment feature is not pricing authority.

## Required Policy Gates

Express payment cannot become buyer-facing language unless these gates are
explicitly reviewed:

- pricing authority;
- collection authority;
- refund and missed-window behavior;
- equity and access policy;
- service boundary and legal-SLA holds;
- revenue, ROI, and procurement-readiness boundary.

## Buyer-Safe Close

Use this language:

> We can simulate whether operators would value faster or verified service, and
> we can show the policy gates needed before any price, collection, refund, or
> revenue language becomes real.

Do not say ROUTE can sell toll access, collect payments, create guaranteed fast
lanes, prove revenue, prove ROI, define equity compliance, or approve public
launch.

## Boundary

This is an express-service simulation and policy ledger. It is not pricing
authority, a payment processor, a tariff, a tolling plan, a legal SLA, a refund
policy, a revenue forecast, ROI proof, procurement readiness, equity approval,
public-readiness proof, or traffic-control authority.
