# ROUTE Industry And Stakeholder Fixture Closeout Report

## Purpose

This report closes the current industry/stakeholder source fixture campaign for
internal communications use.

It answers one narrow question: what does the nine-fixture package prove about
ROUTE's story, and what does it still not prove?

This is not an external validation record. It does not create industry
validation, stakeholder endorsement, agency review, official-plan status,
construction readiness, guaranteed service, numeric ROI, funding eligibility,
compliance, public readiness, external readiness, approval, or broad acceptance.

## Current Decision

Decision: **fixture_package_populated_external_validation_held**

ROUTE now has nine bounded internal fixtures covering terminal access, freight
operations, rural/agricultural access, state delivery controls,
community/environmental concerns, non-driving access, resilience/emergency
management, ROI/cost discipline, and technical rehearsal controls.

The package can safely say:

- "nine bounded source-backed fixtures exist";
- "the major review lanes are represented and source-gated";
- "each fixture records what it still refuses to claim";
- "external validation remains held."

The package cannot safely say:

- "industry validated the plan";
- "stakeholders endorsed the plan";
- "a state or agency approved the plan";
- "the package is ready for public or external use";
- "the fixtures prove construction, service, ROI, compliance, eligibility, or
  funding readiness."

## Allowed Language

Use:

- "The package contains nine bounded source-backed fixtures."
- "The fixtures demonstrate source custody, role review, and held-claim
  discipline across the major review lanes."
- "The lanes are represented and source-gated."
- "The fixtures are internal rehearsal examples, not external validation."
- "External use requires venue-specific source custody, role review,
  prohibited-claim scan, L0, and closeout."

Avoid:

- "validated by industry";
- "stakeholder-approved";
- "agency-reviewed";
- "ready to build";
- "public-ready";
- "external-ready";
- "proves ROI";
- "eligible for funding";
- "cleared for compliance";
- "guaranteed service."

## Gate

Decision: **pass_with_risk for internal fixture package; hold external validation**

Rationale: STAKE-FIX-001 through STAKE-FIX-009 close the current fixture
campaign for internal rehearsal and media-safe reference. The fixtures prove
that ROUTE can represent stakeholder/industry lanes with source custody, role
review, and explicit holds. They do not prove that any external industry,
stakeholder, state, agency, community, or funder has validated the plan.
