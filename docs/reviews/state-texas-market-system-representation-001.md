# Texas Market-System Representation 001

## Scope

This refinement raises the Texas map from a Triangle-plus-branches view to a
statewide market-system discovery surface.

The update adds Waco, Lubbock, McAllen, Victoria, Tyler, and Eagle Pass, and
adds service promises for Central Texas, South Plains/Panhandle, Rio Grande
Valley, East Texas, Gulf evacuation/port access, and secondary border gateways.

## Rationale

A state client will not accept a map that treats its service system as one
dominant line plus feeders. Texas has a statewide requirement to connect its
largest metros, ports, border gateways, energy regions, plains cities, and
regional markets in the smartest way possible.

The updated T1 posture therefore includes more major city and gateway promises
while preserving evidence boundaries.

## Held Claims

The map does not claim official TxDOT priority, legal SLA, construction
readiness, cost, numeric ROI, funding eligibility, compliance, endorsement,
external validation, or public readiness.
