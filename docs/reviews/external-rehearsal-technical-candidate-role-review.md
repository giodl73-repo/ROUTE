# External Rehearsal Technical Candidate Role Review

## Scope

This review reruns the selected `.roles` lanes after the FHWA/USDOT-style
technical candidate packet received its technical rude Q&A addendum.

Reviewed inputs:

- `docs/reviews/external-rehearsal-packet-candidate-001.md`
- `docs/reviews/external-rehearsal-technical-rude-qa-addendum.md`
- the linked evidence posture, demo capture, source operations, optimizer,
  graph/scoring, release scope, fixture closeout, and presenter-control surfaces

This is an internal role review only. It is not a real FHWA, USDOT, state DOT,
sponsor, or technical reviewer meeting. It does not create external validation,
endorsement, official-plan status, construction readiness, guaranteed service,
numeric ROI, eligibility, compliance, public readiness, agency approval, or
external readiness.

## Overall Decision

Decision: **pass_with_risk_for_internal_technical_candidate; hold_external_rehearsal**

Rationale: The candidate and addendum are suitable for another internal
technical rehearsal because they keep the ask on evidence paths, source
custody, command capture, optimizer lineage, graph/scoring assumptions,
release-scope boundaries, and held claims. They are not suitable for external
use until a named venue, reviewer class, presenter, recorder, selected final
packet, venue-specific source custody, prohibited-claim scan, L0, and any
required L1/L2 evidence close.

## Claims Approved For Internal Candidate Use

- ROUTE can show a candidate technical reviewer how claim labels, sources,
  command capture, optimizer artifacts, graph/scoring assumptions, and held
  claims are organized.
- The FHWA/USDOT-style technical lane is a reasonable first internal candidate
  because it asks what can be inspected, reproduced, challenged, or held.
- The technical rude Q&A addendum is the required hostile-question prep sheet
  for this candidate before a venue-specific packet is filled.
- External rehearsal remains held until the packet is made concrete and
  validated by repo-local gates for the selected use.

## Claims Not Approved

- FHWA, USDOT, a state DOT, a sponsor, or an outside technical reviewer has
  reviewed, accepted, approved, endorsed, or validated ROUTE.
- The current packet is ready for a real external rehearsal.
- Any map proves service, construction, upgrade, public-readiness, or release
  readiness.
- Any demo, fixture, score, appendix, or Q&A answer proves operating
  reliability, design adequacy, managed-lane performance, final route ranking,
  funding eligibility, compliance, numeric ROI, or a business case.
