# R1 - Freight Economist

Verdict: **hold**

## Findings

- **BLOCK** - Zero demand and throughput provide no basis for delay, inventory,
  reliability, or capital-benefit claims.
- **BLOCK** - Snapshot observations cannot support annual failure probability.
- **WARN** - The intervention did not model physically independent paths.
- **NOTE** - A flagship choice without a common comparative demand screen risks
  opportunity-cost error.

## Disposition

All cost, NPV, benefit, and capital-ranking claims remain prohibited. The next
gate is a nonzero source-labeled demand fixture and explicit alternatives
comparison.
