\begin{abstract}
Interstate 2.0 is a targeted upgrade of the existing US interstate system, not a new network. Using the ROUTE corpus of 227 scored corridors and five tracks of analysis (corpus calibration, gap identification, freight economics, climate exposure, and I2.0 design), we synthesize a complete design framework and investment portfolio. The framework has six investment components: managed freight lanes (\$121B), diamond interchange upgrades (\$4.5B), compound exposure hardening (\$18B), missing link construction (\$88B), intermodal integration (\$12B), and EV charging infrastructure (\$8B). Total capital: \$253 billion over 30 years, with aggregate NPV of \$298 billion at a 7\% discount rate (benefit-cost ratio 2.2:1). A transit integration layer (\$2B) unlocks 9 T1/T1 diamond hubs for intercity bus service at 1\% of total program cost. The framework reduces national freight reliability variance by an estimated 40\%, reduces transcontinental transit time from 4.5 to 3.5 days, and hardens the five highest-risk compound exposure corridors against climate-driven closures.
\end{abstract}
