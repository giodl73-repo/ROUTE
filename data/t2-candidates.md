# Interstate 2.0 — T2 Candidate List

**Date**: 2026-05-07  
**Rubric**: v1.2 (but T2 determined primarily by FUNCTION, not score)

## T2 Classification Framework

T2 is determined by passing at least one of three screening tests:
- **Relief Valve (RV)**: Closure materially worsens a T1 corridor's congestion
- **Bridge (BR)**: Connects two T1 corridors where no T1 directly does; backtrack > 100mi without it  
- **Gateway (GW)**: Primary freight on-ramp for metro > 1M to the T1 network

Score threshold for existing interstates: ≥ 11/150 (STRAHNET baseline plus some operational signal).  
Score threshold for US highway upgrades: ≥ 11/150 AND passes one screening test.

2. Full alignment ≥ 200 miles
3. Interstate-upgradeable (terrain, right-of-way, access control feasible)

## T2 Standards (reminder from tier-standards.md)

- PTI target: ≤ 1.30
- EV charging: ≥ 100kW every 75 miles
- Rest areas: 150 miles, 20+ truck spaces, enhanced
- Transit: regional stops at T1/T2 interchanges
- Bus frequency: ≤ 4 hour headways
- Resilience spurs: every 75 miles rural
- Bridge target: fair+ by 2035
- Capacity expansion: only where V/C > 0.90
