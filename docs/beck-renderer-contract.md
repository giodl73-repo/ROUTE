# Beck Renderer Contract

## Purpose

ROUTE uses Beck-style maps to make the highway system read like a transit
network: stops, transfers, promise corridors, and service tiers should be
legible before geography is literal.

The renderer is allowed to distort distance and direction. It is not allowed to
distort topology.

This document owns the contract between optimizer artifacts and schematic map
generation. The map should eventually consume selected route, stop, contact,
and service-column artifacts directly instead of relying on hand-authored line
chains.

## Source Order

The Beck renderer must prefer generated optimizer artifacts in this order:

1. `data/t1-line-selector.csv`
2. `data/t1-stop-selector.csv`
3. `data/t1-topology-repairs.csv`
4. `data/t1-beck-alignment.csv`
5. `data/t2-regionalizer.csv`
6. `data/t2-service-selection.csv`
7. `data/t1-feedback-docket.csv`
8. `data/optimizer-map-hooks.csv`
9. hand-authored Beck fixtures, only as compatibility fallbacks

If a hand-authored fixture disagrees with an optimizer artifact, the renderer
must emit a diagnostic row rather than silently choosing the fixture.

## Topology Rules

1. A line may only bend at a selected stop, selected transfer, or explicit
   schematic bend stop.
2. A line may not pass through a stop without recording whether it stops,
   bypasses, or needs repair.
3. Two lines may only transfer where the optimizer records a real contact.
4. Near-miss geometry is not a transfer.
5. A same-color route may not loop over itself unless a selected loop service
   column explicitly authorizes that behavior.
6. Parallel T2 lines with the same parent trunk color must either separate
   visually or be merged/demoted by the T2 service selector.
7. A lower-tier connector may not terminate near a higher-tier route without a
   selected contact or explicit terminal exception.

These rules apply equally to national maps, T1 regional maps, T2-only maps, and
T3 zone maps.

## Geometry Rules

Beck geometry should be generated from stop order, contacts, and schematic
constraints:

- Preserve route stop order from one endpoint to the other.
- Condense long rural gaps into schematic intervals.
- Expand tight metro areas when many stops, transfers, or T2 contacts compete
  for space.
- Prefer 0, 45, and 90 degree segments when they preserve stop/contact truth.
- Add a bend only by selecting or reusing a stop that can own the bend.
- Keep labels attached to selected stops, not arbitrary route midpoints.

The renderer can move stops for legibility, but it cannot move the obligation.
If a bend, transfer, or label needs a node, the optimizer must select that node
or record a repair.

## Tier Rendering Semantics

| Tier | Renderer duty | Forbidden shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Show national promise spine, transfer hubs, and selected major stops | Drawing a national trunk that lacks selected stops or SLA lineage |
| T2 | Show regional service columns, parent-trunk color lineage, and real T1/T2 contacts | Ending thin lines near T1 without a contact stop |
| T3 | Show zone feeder chains plus local T1/T2 context | Rendering standalone horizontal placeholders as final maps |
| T4 | Show local access only inside a zone or scenario context | Promoting local access onto the national schematic |

T2 color is inherited from service lineage, not decoration. If a T2 route serves
two T1 trunks, the renderer may split the line color at a selected transfer or
use a gradient only when the service-selection artifact records the parent
lineage clearly enough to audit.

## Diagnostics

Every Beck render family should have a machine-readable diagnostic surface.

Required diagnostic classes:

- `missing-selected-stop`
- `unstopped-transfer`
- `near-miss-contact`
- `bend-without-stop`
- `route-order-mismatch`
- `duplicate-service-loop`
- `parallel-service-conflict`
- `missing-parent-color`
- `fallback-fixture-used`

Diagnostics should point to the next artifact, not just name the defect.

## Map Atlas Contract

`data/map-atlas.csv` owns render commands, expected dimensions, byte-size smoke
checks, tier role, and game use. The atlas verifies that maps exist and are
large enough to be plausible outputs. It does not prove topology correctness.

Topology correctness belongs to Beck diagnostics and optimizer alignment gates.

## Current Status

Current maps are partially optimizer-gated:

- T1 stop chains are generated by `data/t1-stop-selector.csv`.
- T1 Beck coverage is checked by `data/t1-beck-alignment.csv`.
- T2 service choices flow through `data/t2-service-selection.csv`.
- Map consumers are listed by `data/optimizer-map-hooks.csv`.

The remaining gap is direct rendering from optimizer-selected stops and service
columns. Until that is implemented, Beck maps should be treated as alignment
checked, not fully optimizer-rendered.
