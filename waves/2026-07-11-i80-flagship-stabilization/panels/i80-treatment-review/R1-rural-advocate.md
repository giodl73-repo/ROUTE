# R1 - Rural Advocate

Verdict: **hold**

## Findings

- **BLOCK** - The package had no node-specific agricultural freight, rural
  access, diversion-burden, or community-benefit gate.
- **WARN** - Emergency diversion could place heavy freight on peri-urban and
  farm-access roads without capacity or clearance proof.
- **NOTE** - Corridor-level agricultural scores do not establish local benefit.

## Disposition

The validation plan now requires farm-to-market use, emergency detour burden,
truck suitability, and a net community-benefit statement.
