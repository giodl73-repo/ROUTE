## Module-Level PP Items

### PP1 — Blocking

**PP1.1 — E.2 NPV arithmetic reconciliation** *(Neumark, Puentes)*
Add a benefit reconciliation table to E.2 Section 3 showing how each component's annual benefit contributes to the claimed $31.2B/yr total that discounts to $298B over 30 years at 7%. The arithmetic must close explicitly; currently it cannot be verified.

**PP1.2 — F.1/F.2 proximity-access reframing** *(Walker, Schmitt)*
Reframe "within 30 miles" as proximity not operational access throughout F.1 and F.2. Add a Limitations paragraph in each paper: "This analysis measures geographic proximity to hub locations; operational access for transit-dependent travelers also requires local feeder service (estimated $500-800M, not included in hub increment cost)." The 14× efficiency finding remains valid; the 12.4M figure needs the proximity qualifier.

**PP1.3 — D.1 composite metric resolution** *(McKinnon 2/4)*
Either (a) restrict D1 to SFHA-based flood scoring and move winter/tornado/wildfire to a separate D1b subscript, or (b) normalize both components to expected annual lane-closure-hours per 100 miles before combining. Option (b) is methodologically superior. Document as a v1.3 amendment under the forward-only protocol.

**PP1.4 — C.1 BPR extrapolation acknowledgment** *(Elefteriadou 2/4)*
Add to C.1 Section 3: "The BPR function was calibrated for V/C ≤ 1.3; at V/C = 1.86 (Bay Area segments), PTI estimates are extrapolated and likely understate actual unreliability. Available NPMRDS probe-vehicle data suggests PTI ≥ 2.0 for these segments, validating the order of magnitude while noting the model limitation."

### PP2 — Important

**PP2.1 — Legislative pathway for F.1/F.2 EIT designation** *(Puentes)*
Replace "Essential Intercity Transportation (EIT) designation" with "a designation analogous to Essential Air Service (49 U.S.C. § 41731), requiring new authorization." Reference 49 U.S.C. § 5311(f) as the closest existing authority.

**PP2.2 — F.2 stop-penalty correction** *(Walker)*
Revise F.2 travel time model: add stop_penalty = N_stops × 8 min, where N ≈ distance/150 miles. For NYC-Chicago (780 miles): 5 intermediate stops × 8 min = 40 min → effective average 58.2 mph not 62 mph. Revise all 12 corridor estimates.

**PP2.3 — Decarbonization thread in E.1/E.2** *(McKinnon — consistent across papers)*
Add one paragraph to E.2 Section 2 on EV compatibility: managed freight lanes are designed for EV trucking (DCFC ≤ 50 mi spacing); the $8B EV component funds infrastructure needed for 2030-2035 EV adoption curve; no incremental cost beyond Component 6.

**PP2.4 — B.3 Donner NPV waiting-cost correction** *(Elefteriadou)*
Correct waiting cost rate from $225/hr to ~$91/hr (idle: driver + fuel at idle + overhead). Recalculate Donner tunnel NPV. Result: NPV drops from ~$15.8B to ~$11.6B. Investment priority ordering is unchanged.

**PP2.5 — Cross-track dependency documentation** *(Adamic, Hanson)*
Add \citep{ROUTE_D1} in B.3 Section 2 where D1 scores are first referenced. Add to MODULE.md Track B: "B.3's compound exposure analysis requires D.1's D1 scores; D.1 must be finalized (PP1.3) before B.3 re-runs corpus."

**PP2.6 — Equity thread consistency** *(Schmitt)*
Ensure F.1 Section 6, F.2 Section 6, and E.2 Section 6 all use consistent ACS B08201 (2022 vintage) data and report the same r=0.68 C3-alignment coefficient. E.2 Section 6 should add a sentence connecting C3-corridor alignment to F.1's hub analysis rather than asserting equity abstractly.
