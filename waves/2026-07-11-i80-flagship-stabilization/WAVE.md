# I-80 Flagship Stabilization

## Mission

Complete one source-to-decision ROUTE anchor that a transportation practitioner
can inspect without learning the entire internal artifact system.

## Opening Rule

No new geographic expansion, international adapter, doctrine family, or
placeholder-only evidence ledger is in scope. Work must strengthen the I-80
flagship, its reproducibility, or its external review path.

## Done Criteria

- The I-80 corridor record is reviewed and contains no annotation placeholders.
- Every material number has a source or an explicit estimate/hold label.
- The selected treatment is traceable to a corridor-specific gap finding.
- Parliament and editorial review change or affirm the treatment and claims.
- A compact packet regenerates from named commands and supports a ten-minute
  presentation.
- External review lanes and questions are ready.
- The flagship command path is reproducible from a clean clone.

## Non-Goals

- No official-plan, construction-readiness, guaranteed-SLA, or positive-ROI
  claim.
- No new national tier doctrine.
- No new international or state adapter.
- No broad rewrite of unrelated T2/T3/T4 ledgers.
- No resolution of unrelated `data/t1-design-review.csv` worktree state.
